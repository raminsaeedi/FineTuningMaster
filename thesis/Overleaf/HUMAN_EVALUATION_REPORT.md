## Aggregation

For each method and dimension, ratings were averaged within each brief and then averaged equally across the eight briefs. This prevents briefs with more rating pages from receiving greater weight. Method contrasts are descriptive because raters were shared across an incomplete-block assignment and the brief count is small. Ordinal Krippendorff’s alpha is reported separately by dimension.

All means are on a 1–5 scale. Retrieval-only (B) has the strongest average profile in this sample, while prompt-only (A) is marginally higher for layout and interaction. Fine-tuned conditions do not exceed B on any dimension. Low agreement on five dimensions, convenience recruitment, eight briefs, and incomplete three-page coverage preclude confirmatory or population-level method rankings.
