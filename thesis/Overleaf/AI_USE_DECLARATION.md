# AI-Use Declaration

The declaration included in `frontmatter/ai_use.tex` states the final disclosure used in the thesis.

OpenAI Codex supported language revision, LaTeX organization, consistency checks, and the drafting and auditing of analysis code. The controlled dataset-enrichment workflow described in Chapter 4 used the recorded model `gpt-5.6-luna`; its outputs remain labelled as LLM-generated and not expert gold. AI systems were not used as scientific sources. The author selected the research design, executed and checked the empirical work, verified claims against stored artifacts and cited literature, and remains responsible for the submitted text and conclusions.

The release procedure used the Scientific Writing, Scientific Critical Thinking, and Scientific Humanizer skills. The procedural methods resource associated with the first two skills is Kassis, Agarwal, He, Patel, and Brueckner (2026), “Scientific Agent Skills: A Library of Procedural Knowledge for Research Agents,” arXiv:2609.00065, DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.00065. It documents editorial procedure and is not evidence for an empirical thesis claim.
